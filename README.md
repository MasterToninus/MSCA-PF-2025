### 1. Excellence

Score: 4.40 / 5 (Weight: 50%)

* The proposal did not fully demonstrate that all aims are clearly achievable.
* The methodology was considered not entirely convincing.
* Key technical challenges were not sufficiently articulated.
* The supervisor’s postdoctoral mentoring experience was not described in enough detail.
* Training activities were not sufficiently specified.

### 2. Impact

Score: 3.70 / 5 (Weight: 30%)


* Career development activities were not described with enough operational detail.
* Target audiences for dissemination and communication were insufficiently specified.
* The broader scientific impact beyond the project duration was assessed as Fair.
* Long-term impact on the research community was not convincingly articulated.
* Impact was the comparatively weakest section of the proposal.

### 3. Implementation

Score: 4.80 / 5 (Weight: 20%)

* The Gantt chart did not clearly indicate when project outcomes would be presented at conferences or seminars.
* Risk mitigation strategy was considered not fully convincing.
* Administrative risks were not sufficiently detailed.



## Overall Assessment (AI recap)

The proposal was considered scientifically strong, ambitious, and well structured. The main areas identified for improvement concern:

Greater methodological clarity and explicit identification of technical challenges.

More detailed description of supervision and training.

Stronger articulation of long-term scientific impact.

More concrete and operational career development measures.

With a total score of 85.40%, the proposal was competitive within the EF-MAT panel but did not receive funding due to high competition and limited budget.
